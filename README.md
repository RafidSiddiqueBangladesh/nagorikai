# Nagorik AI (নাগরিক AI)
**Every Citizen. Every Right. Every Voice.**

Nagorik AI is an inclusive, AI-powered civic ecosystem designed to make public services, legal information, government resources, and civic participation accessible to every person in Bangladesh—regardless of language, location, gender, age, education, or physical ability.

Through one unified platform, citizens can ask questions in Bangla or English, use voice-based assistance, understand government documents, discover public services, find legal support, report community problems, and participate in local decision-making. Its AI assistant guides people through essential services such as NID registration and correction, birth certificates, disability and elderly allowances, trade licences, agricultural support, and other government procedures with clear instructions and document checklists.

Nagorik AI puts accessibility at its core. It aims to support Bangladeshi Sign Language communication, Braille conversion, screen readers, voice navigation, and eye-controlled communication for people with limited mobility. Dedicated services will also help women seeking safety and legal support, Indigenous communities in the Chittagong Hill Tracts seeking information in their languages, journalists accessing public-interest resources, and underserved communities facing barriers to public services.

The platform connects citizens with local government offices, municipalities, Union Parishads, hospitals, legal professionals, community events, development projects, public notices, and verified information. Its transparency and citizen-participation tools enable people to raise concerns, submit community proposals, follow complaints, examine public project information, and take part in consultations.

By combining AI assistance, accessibility technology, civic engagement, local information, and community collaboration, Nagorik AI aims to transform how people understand their rights, access services, and make their voices heard.

Our vision is simple: a Bangladesh where no citizen is excluded from information, opportunity, public services, or the right to be heard.
